# Code of Conduct

NullNoise welcomes contributions from people with different backgrounds and
levels of experience. Participants are expected to help keep project spaces
respectful, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussion and review.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Welcome questions and differing viewpoints in good faith.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's recordings, personal
  information, or private communications without permission.
- Focus discussions on improving the project and its users' experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized conduct,
deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publishing private information without
consent are not acceptable. Repeated disruptive behavior or intentionally
derailing project discussions may also be addressed.

## Enforcement and reporting

Project maintainers may remove or edit contributions, issue a warning, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation based on the severity and
context of a violation.

To report a conduct concern, contact a project maintainer privately through
their GitHub profile. Do not report sensitive conduct details in a public
issue. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical.

This policy applies to project repositories, issue and pull-request
discussions, and project-related community spaces.
